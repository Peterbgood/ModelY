How to read this

  1. Under is good. It means you’ve driven less than your pace allows, so you have spare miles.
  2. Watch the “per day from now on” number. Staying at or under it keeps you inside the limit to the very end.
  3. The pace warning matters most. Even if you’re under today, a high daily pace can still put you over by Jan 2028.
